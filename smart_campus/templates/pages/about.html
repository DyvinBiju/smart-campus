About SmartCampus

Campus Operations & Resource Management System

Project Mission

SmartCampus is a comprehensive web-based platform designed to centralize and automate campus operations, asset tracking, maintenance workflows, inventory management, and reporting analytics across educational institutions.

Built on Django, PostgreSQL, Docker, and standard modular architecture, SmartCampus enables campus administrators and facility teams to maintain operational efficiency and resource transparency.

Core System Modules

  • Asset Management: Real-time tracking of campus equipment, locations, categories, and operational status.
  • User & Authentication: Secure role-based user access, authentication, and profile controls.
  • Complaint & Maintenance: Streamlined ticketing for campus repairs and facility requests.
  • Inventory & Resources: Stock monitoring, material requisitions, and supply logs.
  • Dashboard & Analytics: Visual reporting and operational intelligence.
